SuperSpellCheck

The spell checker that reads the whole sentence.

SuperSpellCheck fixes typos using the words around them, catches correctly spelled wrong words that other checkers miss, and stays quiet when your writing is fine. It runs on your device, so your text stays private.

Add to your browser, free See the benchmark
I got a letter form the bank about my acount. It don't make sense, so I should of called them. Each underline here is a real result. “form” → “from” and “don't” → “doesn't” are spelled correctly, so a dictionary checker like Chrome's misses them.

Private by default

The engine is compiled to WebAssembly and runs inside the extension. Nothing you type leaves your browser.

Context-aware

A language model ranks corrections by how well they fit the sentence, not just by how close they are to what you typed.

Quiet

Names, code, URLs and @mentions are left alone. Ignore a suggestion twice and you won't see it again.

Works where you write

Text areas, rich editors, email and comment boxes, in Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari.
